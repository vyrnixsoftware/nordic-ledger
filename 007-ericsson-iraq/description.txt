On 27 February 2022 the International Consortium of Investigative Journalists and Swedish television published the Ericsson List: a leaked internal report showing that Sweden's most famous engineering company had, between 2011 and 2019, paid tens of millions of dollars in Iraq through sham contracts, cash-carrying suppliers and fixers, and had bought passage for its equipment along transport routes controlled by Islamic State. Two years earlier Ericsson had paid the United States 1.06 billion dollars for bribery in Djibouti, China, Vietnam, Indonesia and Kuwait, and promised to come clean. It had not mentioned Iraq. This is the Ericsson bribery ledger: the 2019 deferred prosecution agreement, the Speedway, Börje Ekholm and the Wallenbergs, the 2022 shareholder revolt, the 2023 guilty plea and 206 million dollar penalty, the monitor, the Swedish acquittals, the Vonage write-downs, and where the company stands in August 2026, as Ekholm prepares to leave.
