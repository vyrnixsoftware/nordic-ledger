# Sponsor outreach — The Nordic Ledger

## 1. Media kit outline (one PDF, 4–6 pages, plus a one-page summary)

1. **Cover** — channel name, tagline ("How European companies rise, fall and get caught"), one hero thumbnail grid, contact email.
2. **What we make** — format (12–25 min documentaries, fortnightly), tone (calm, precise, dry British narration), editorial standards (source lists, corrections policy, human-edited / AI-assisted disclosure). Two or three flagship episode links (Northvolt, Wirecard, Nokia).
3. **Audience** — subscribers `{{SUBS}}`, monthly views `{{VIEWS_30D}}`, average view duration `{{AVD}}` / retention `{{RET_%}}`, top countries (`{{US %}}`, `{{UK %}}`, `{{DE %}}`, `{{Nordics %}}`), age (`{{25–44 %}}`), gender split, device split. Note the audience profile: finance-literate professionals, founders, students, investors.
4. **Why us** — long watch time on evergreen topics; brand-safe, no outrage bait; European business audience that is hard to reach through US creators; every video keeps earning views for years (show 30/90/365-day view curves for one episode).
5. **Sponsorship formats & rates**
   - Integrated 60–90 s mid-roll read (host-scripted, AI narrated with brand approval): `{{RATE_MIDROLL}}`
   - Pre-roll 30 s mention: `{{RATE_PREROLL}}`
   - Description link + pinned comment add-on: `{{RATE_LINK}}`
   - Series package (3 or 6 episodes): `{{RATE_PACKAGE}}` (–15/–25 %)
   - Dedicated explainer / co-produced episode: on request
   - Basis: CPM `{{CPM}}` on projected 30-day views `{{PROJ_VIEWS}}`; guarantee: `{{VIEW_GUARANTEE}}` or make-good.
6. **Past partners / testimonials** — `{{LOGOS}}` (or "Launch partners wanted — introductory rates" if none).
7. **Process** — brief → script draft for approval (one revision round) → published with FTC/ASA-compliant disclosure → performance report at 7 and 30 days.
8. **Contact** — `{{NAME}}`, `{{EMAIL}}`, `{{CALENDLY}}`, channel URL.

Keep a live Google Sheet with stats and update the kit monthly.
