# The Nordic Ledger — YouTube channel settings

## About (≤1000 characters)

The Nordic Ledger is an independent, research-based documentary channel from the Nordics. We tell the stories of how European and Nordic companies rise, fall and get caught — money laundering at Danske Bank, the collapse of Northvolt, the fraud at Wirecard, the long decline of Nokia — with calm, precise narration and a dry sense of humour.

How we work: every script is researched, written and edited with human editorial judgement, using AI tools to assist with research, drafting and production. The narration uses an AI-generated voice. Every video lists its sources in the description, and we correct errors publicly when we get something wrong.

New documentaries every fortnight. Not financial advice — just the ledger, honestly kept.
