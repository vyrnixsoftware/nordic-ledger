How a Swedish startup founded by two ex-Tesla executives raised roughly $15 billion from Volkswagen, Goldman Sachs and Nordic pension funds, built a gigafactory near the Arctic Circle, shipped less than half a percent of its promised output, and became the largest bankruptcy in modern Swedish history.
