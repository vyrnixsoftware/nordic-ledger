Everyone knows Nokia missed the iPhone. Fewer know that at its peak Nokia was four percent of Finland's GDP, a fifth of its exports and nearly a quarter of its corporate tax, and that when the handset business collapsed, Finland did not. Around 1,000 startups from one severance scheme, Supercell, Wolt, a €2.85 billion games industry, and a €20 billion networks company few people notice: this is the Finnish-insider account of the rarest thing in business, a national champion whose fall left the country better off.
